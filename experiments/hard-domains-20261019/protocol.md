These are seeded benchmark defects, not historical product defects. No operational data/devices/services/DB are touched.

Source-A freeze and every source-B byte are checked before runs.

A single success is a heuristic, not established difficulty. Invalid infrastructure is not difficulty and remains recorded.

Keep all failures and null effects; never replace bad outcomes. Screen and main samples are never pooled.
