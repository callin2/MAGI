# Repair and validation

Implemented an exact global search over chronologically ordered actual-start events, with room choice and skip branches. A selected meeting suppresses its other fold occurrence. Dependencies must already be selected and finished. Room occupancy and per-person last selected meeting are maintained independently; directed setup applies only to consecutive selected meetings, so a useful intermediate meeting is not incorrectly rejected by an all-pairs constraint. Suffix value/count upper bounds safely prune branches. Equal objectives use explicit code-unit tuple comparison, not locale collation.

Civil labels are resolved using Intl zone offsets sampled on both sides of the label and verified by round-trip civil formatting. This covers the supported zones' 2024–2026 transitions, including Lord Howe's half-hour shift and Kathmandu's quarter-hour offset. Durations are elapsed milliseconds. Range checks and room blocks use half-open intervals. Inputs are not modified and no calendar, network, or external service is accessed.

Validation: ran the provided tests and candidate.test.mjs through the tests tool; 10 tests passed, 0 failed. Candidate coverage includes global value/count optimization, alternate room assignment, ASCII ties, dependencies, input preservation, range and block endpoints, directed nonmetric consecutive transitions, folds in three DST zones, nonexistent starts, Kathmandu, and dependency-driven fold selection. Fifty deterministic five-meeting cases were compared against a separate exhaustive whole-assignment oracle that checks transitions only after selection.

Limits: exact search is exponential, appropriate to the stated maximum of ten meetings and three rooms. Civil-time resolution relies on the host Node/Intl IANA time-zone data and is scoped to the five specified zones and 2024–2026; arbitrary historical zones/rules are not supported by contract. Inputs are assumed well formed, as specified. Tests are representative plus small exhaustive comparisons, not exhaustive over every permitted input.
