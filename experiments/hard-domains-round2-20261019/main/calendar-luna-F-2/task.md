Repair solution.mjs for a synthetic meeting assignment optimizer. Picking individually attractive slots can miss a better whole schedule, and applying simple interval adjacency to people who need room-transition setup produces infeasible bookings. This computes recommendations only; do not access a live calendar.

Export book({range_start,range_end,rooms,transitions,meetings,blocks}). rooms is an array of distinct ASCII room IDs. transitions[from][to] is a nonnegative integer setup duration in minutes for a person moving between consecutive selected meetings; the table is directed, need not be metric, and has0 diagonal. It applies only to immediately consecutive meetings for that person, not every earlier/later pair. A selected intermediate meeting can change which transition is relevant. Room busy blocks have {room_id,start,end} as absolute RFC3339 instants and do not themselves involve a person.

Each meeting has {id,zone,local_start,minutes,value,eligible_rooms,people,requires}. IDs are distinct ASCII strings. local_start is civil YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm in zone; supported zones are UTC,America/New_York,Europe/Berlin,Asia/Kathmandu,Australia/Lord_Howe in2024–2026. fold is an additional required field, earlier|later|either. Nonexistent starts have no option. For an ambiguous start, earlier/later choose that instant; either offers both alternatives, but at most ONE occurrence of this meeting may be booked. A normal start offers one. minutes is positive elapsed duration<=120; value is a nonnegative integer. eligible_rooms and people are lists without duplicates. requires is a list of other meeting IDs and forms an acyclic graph.

A booking picks one offered instant and one eligible room. Its entire elapsed interval must fit [range_start,range_end]. Selected intervals in the same room, or sharing any person, must not overlap. All intervals are half-open so equality at endpoints is allowed unless the selected person's consecutive-room transition requires a positive gap. For each person's selected meetings in chronological order, the next start must be at least previous end plus transitions[previous_room][next_room]. Each required meeting must also be selected and end no later than its dependent meeting's start. Room blocks prevent only overlapping occupancy in that room; they impose no person/setup rule.

Among feasible schedules, maximize total value, then booked count, then choose the lexicographically smallest sequence of (meeting_id,room_id,start,end), sorted by meeting_id. Compare strings by ASCII/code units; timestamps are UTC ISO strings with milliseconds. Return exactly {value,count,bookings}, where each booking is {meeting_id,room_id,start,end}. Bounds: at most10 meetings,3 rooms, query span<=7 days, all inputs well formed and references known. Empty schedule is valid. Preserve inputs.
